# Messages

Use for email, team messages, requests, and status updates. This is practical editorial guidance; the research reference does not establish an optimal email template.

## Decisions

For a request, make the requested action or decision easy to find. Include the reason and deadline only when supplied or authorized. Preserve a courteous greeting, sign-off, or softener when it serves the relationship. Politeness is not automatically filler.

For an update, distinguish what happened, what is still uncertain or blocked, and the next action already stated in the source. Match the recipient's context: a teammate may need an identifier; a stakeholder may need its consequence. Do not replace known details with generic business language.

Use a subject line for a new email when helpful. Do not fabricate a subject, signature, deadline, or promise while editing only an existing paragraph. Keep short messages short, without imposing headings or a repeated closing summary.

Finish when the reader can tell why they received the message, what action is requested if any, and the stated timing or uncertainty. An informational message need not invent a call to action.

## Example: concise and courteous

Input:

> Hi Maya, I just wanted to reach out to ask whether you could possibly review the migration plan by Thursday at 14:00 CEST. We need your approval before we can schedule the rollout. Thanks, Sam.

Possible edit:

> Hi Maya, could you review the migration plan by Thursday at 14:00 CEST? We need your approval before scheduling the rollout. Thanks, Sam.

The deadline, approval dependency, and politeness survive. “Approve the plan by Thursday” would change a review request into an instruction to approve.

## Example: already effective

> Thanks, Lena. Could you check the attachment when you have a moment? I’m unsure whether the totals include VAT.

Return unchanged for a general polish request. Turning it into “Check the VAT totals” loses uncertainty and changes the relationship. A request for a different tone would justify another edit.
